How Kevin works

Kevin is hands-on. He scopes engagements directly with the customer, owns the architecture and eval design, and stays accountable through production launch. For longer engagements he assembles a dedicated team of senior AI engineers around a single point of accountability — him.

What he refuses: vague pilots, demo-grade systems shipped to real users, governance bolted on after launch, and "innovation theatre" that doesn't move a real workflow metric.

How we work

Three principles that shape every engagement.

Production from day one

Every system we build is designed for thousands of repeated runs — with evals, monitoring, fallback paths, and runbooks. No demo-grade code in production paths.

Governance baked in

Audit trails, RBAC, escalation protocols, and security review are designed into the architecture, not bolted on. SOC 2 / HIPAA contexts welcomed.

Operator accountability

One senior owner per engagement. We answer to your operating-model goals, not just feature velocity. Engagements close with a real handoff or roll into retainer.

Team Model

A senior owner, plus the right specialists for each engagement.

Enterprise AI Studio operates a partner-and-specialist model. Kevin is the senior owner across every engagement; the specialist team flexes around the work.

Senior AI engineers (specialist bench)

Production AI builders with deep experience in evals, retrieval, agent orchestration, and platform-level reliability. We bring in the right specialist for each engagement rather than billing one shape of engineer for every problem.
