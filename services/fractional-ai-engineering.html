Typical Engagement

How retainers work.

You choose your hours (20/40/60/80/month) and kick off with a brief alignment call. Your engineer then joins your team — attends standup, syncs on priorities, picks up work from your backlog.

Hours are tracked and flexible: slower months roll unused hours forward (up to 2 months), faster months you can burn them or add capacity. You get a monthly report of work completed, current systems status, and recommended priorities.

Critical production issues are handled immediately, outside the hour budget. Everything else comes from your monthly allocation.

FAQ

Common questions.

How many hours per month do I get?

Our retainers are offered as 20, 40, 60, or 80 hours per month. You can flex usage month-to-month, and unused hours roll over (up to 2 months) so you're not penalized for lighter months.

What's included in a retainer?

Dedicated engineering hours, monthly evals, quarterly reviews, on-call coverage for production issues, and ongoing optimization. You get a senior engineer as part of your team.

Can I pause or scale up a retainer?

Yes. You can pause for a month or scale hours up/down with 2 weeks notice. The engagement is designed to flex with your actual needs.

What happens if we hit a production issue?

Retainer hours include on-call coverage. Critical issues get immediate attention; we prioritize your production systems over other work.

Do you work across our whole AI stack, or just new builds?

Both. We build new systems, optimize existing ones, run evals, handle migrations, integrate with your infrastructure, and manage production incidents. You get your own AI team.

Who's the engineer on our team?

A dedicated senior engineer who becomes part of your team. They own your roadmap, understand your systems, and provide continuity across projects.
